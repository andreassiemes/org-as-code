# Organization: Delivery Organization

* [Delivery Organization](/org.md) - Client delivery organization of a mid-size consulting firm.

## Units

* [Delivery Organization](/units/delivery.md) - Strategic steering of the delivery organization: budget, headcount, restructuring, initiative prioritization.

## Roles

* [COO](/roles/coo.md) - Chairs the Steering Committee; final decision authority for delivery-organization matters.
* [Head of Delivery](/roles/head-of-delivery.md) - Drives decision proposals into the Steering Committee and reports delivery metrics.

## Committees

* [Steering Committee](/gremien/steering-committee.md) - Strategic decisions for the delivery organization: budget >10k EUR, headcount, restructuring, prioritization.
* [Delivery Lead Sync](/gremien/delivery-lead-sync.md) - Operational sync of delivery leads; primary feeder of decision requests into the Steering Committee.
